Full text
Understanding the evolution of cooperation in structured populations remains a central challenge in multidisciplinary areas. Full text
Many strategic decisions involve both substantial complexity and time pressure, but the association between decision speed and decision quality of cognitively demanding strategic decisions is not well understood. Full text
Policymakers’ push for higher employment rates has renewed attention to the labour market position of long-term unemployed jobseekers and inactive persons. However, stigma related to unemployment or inactivity can hinder their hiring chances when applying for a job. This systematic literature review investigates whether, when, and why periods of not working are penalized in hiring. Our review confirms employers treat the unemployed and inactive less favourably than their employed counterparts.

Full text
Hydrothermal systems at ultraslow-spreading mid-ocean ridges remain poorly characterized, particularly where sedimentary and ultramafic influences intersect. Major element concentrations, dissolved gases, and thermodynamic modeling are used to investigate fluid-rock interactions.

Paper Picnic is a non-commercial academic project. This notice explains what personal
data the project collects, why, who else handles it, and what rights you have. It is
written to meet the transparency requirements of the UK GDPR.

#### 2. What this notice covers
<br>

Paper Picnic has three parts:
    
- **Everything Politics** — a weekly email with all new articles from Political Science,
  International Relations, and Public Administration.
- **Melting Pot** — a personalised weekly email covering the journals and preprint
  categories you selected.
- **paper-picnic.com** — the public website, including the article archive.


<br>
#### 3. What data is collected, and why
<br>

##### Melting Pot

When you send a subscription request we store your email address, your journal and preprint
category selections, the date you first subscribed, and the date of the last email sent to
you. These are used to deliver your weekly email, to assemble its contents, to record when
you consented, and to avoid sending you the same edition twice.

Your address is checked against a list of known university domains to apply the sign-up
restriction described on the [notifications page]({{ site.baseurl }}/notifications). The
result is not stored.

A new subscription request replaces your previous selections; we keep no history of earlier
ones. No human reads your subscription email, though the maintainer can access stored
records when handling a support request or fixing a fault.

##### Everything Politics

Your email address is added to the announcement list on UCL's Mailman system. Paper Picnic
keeps no separate copy, but as list administrator the maintainer can view subscriber
addresses. Mailman also records technical data such as bounce handling.

##### Website

Nothing on the website requires you to identify yourself: no account, no login, no form.

Cabin, a cookieless analytics service, produces aggregate visitor statistics; it sets no
cookies and stores no IP addresses. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through
Cloudflare, so both see your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser's user
agent. Paper Picnic has no access to those logs.

Your browser stores a few display preferences — theme, journal and preprint visibility and
order, abstract expansion. These never leave your device and clearing your browser data
removes them.

<br>
#### 4. Legal basis
<br>

The legal basis for sending you email is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR). You can
withdraw it at any time — see section 8 — and withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of
anything done before you withdrew. 

Providing your email address is voluntary. There is no contract or legal requirement to
provide it, and the only consequence of not providing it is that we cannot send you the
weekly email.

For website visitors, the legal basis is our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR)
in making the site available and protecting it from abuse. You can object — see section 9.

<br>
#### 5. Who else handles your data
<br>

- **Cloudflare** — United States. Website proxy and DNS, inbound email routing, and
  storage of Melting Pot subscriber records.
- **Plunk (useplunk.com)** — European Union. Sends the weekly Melting Pot emails.
- **University College London** — United Kingdom. Operates the Mailman list for Everything
  Politics under its own policies, not on our instructions.
- **GitHub** — United States. Hosts the website via GitHub Pages.
- **Cabin (withcabin.com)** — European Union. Aggregate website analytics.

<br>
#### 6. International transfers
<br>

Plunk and Cabin process data in the European Economic Area, which is covered by the UK's
adequacy regulations.

Cloudflare and GitHub process data in the United States, which is not. Both are certified
under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and their standard contractual
terms provide for the UK Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback.

Write to <a href="mailto:{{ page.email }}">{{ page.email }}</a> if you would like a copy of
those terms.

<br>
#### 7. How long data is kept
<br>

##### Melting Pot

Your email address and preferences are kept for as long as you remain subscribed. When you
unsubscribe, the record is deleted immediately, and we instruct Plunk to delete its own
contact record at the same time.

Cloudflare's operational logs can briefly contain an email address — for example when a
delivery attempt fails. Cloudflare retains them for at most seven days.

##### Everything Politics

Your address stays on the announcement list until you leave it or the list is closed.
Technical data that Mailman records in running the list is retained under UCL's own rules.

##### Website

Cloudflare and GitHub keep their own access and security logs, each under its own retention
period; Paper Picnic can neither read nor delete them. Cabin holds only aggregate
statistics, with nothing tied to an individual visitor.

#### 9. Your rights
<br>
    
You have the right to:

- **access** the data held about you, and get a copy;
- **correct** it if it is wrong;
- **erase** it;
- **restrict** its processing;
- **portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable form;
- **withdraw consent** at any time;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests (see section 4).

To exercise any of these, write to <a href="mailto:{{ page.email }}">{{ page.email }}</a>. 
There is no charge, and you will get a response within one month.
